PRIVACY POLICY.

LAST UPDATED: 23 NOVEMBER 2026

New Era Logistics respects the privacy of our website visitors, drivers and community members.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected, why it may be used, how it may be protected and what choices you may have regarding your information.

01. Who We Are

New Era Logistics (NEL) is a South African-founded Virtual Trucking Company established in September 2023. We operate as an online gaming and community organisation primarily focused on Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact our management using the contact details at the bottom of this policy.

02. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with New Era Logistics, we may receive or process the following categories of information:

  • Identity information: Gaming usernames, Discord usernames, TruckersMP usernames and Trucksbook usernames.
  • Membership information: Recruitment application details, membership status, VTC roles and department assignments.
  • Communication information: Messages or information you voluntarily provide when contacting our management or staff.
  • Event information: Convoy attendance, participation records and event-related photographs or recordings.
  • Technical information: Information that may be processed by our website hosting provider, such as IP addresses, browser details and server logs.

Our current static website does not itself provide user accounts or a built-in recruitment submission form. If you contact us through an external platform or application form, the information you submit there may be processed separately.

03. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate community purposes, including:

  • Processing and managing VTC recruitment applications.
  • Managing membership, roles and department responsibilities.
  • Organising convoys, events and community activities.
  • Communicating important company announcements.
  • Maintaining community safety and investigating reported misconduct.
  • Maintaining and improving our website and community operations.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

04. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, New Era Logistics aims to process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable privacy laws.

Depending on the circumstances, information may be processed based on your consent, your request to participate in our community, legitimate community administration interests, or an applicable legal obligation.

05. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not currently operate a built-in account system or intentionally use advertising cookies.

Website hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses and server logs for security and operational purposes.

Our website may load third-party resources, including Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests their resources.

06. Third-Party Platforms

New Era Logistics may use external services such as:

  • Discord: Community communication and membership management.
  • TruckersMP: Virtual trucking activities and VTC participation.
  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting.
  • Google Fonts: Website typography.

These services are operated by independent providers. Their own privacy policies and terms govern their handling of information. We encourage users to review those policies before using their services.

07. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with authorised New Era Logistics management or relevant staff when necessary for membership administration, event coordination, safety or community operations.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or to address security incidents and serious misconduct.

08. Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal obligations.

Membership and administrative records may be retained while necessary for VTC operations. When information is no longer required, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or anonymise it where appropriate.

09. Data Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical precautions to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, including POPIA, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally appropriate.
  • Object to certain types of processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with the relevant supervisory authority.

Requests can be submitted using the contact details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. International Users

New Era Logistics welcomes drivers from different countries. Information may be processed by third-party platforms or hosting providers in countries outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, we aim to take reasonable steps to ensure that international processing is handled in accordance with relevant privacy requirements.

12. Children's Privacy

New Era Logistics does not intentionally request personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required. Users must comply with the age requirements of the gaming and communication platforms they use.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact our management so that the matter can be reviewed.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operations, website features or applicable privacy requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

14. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions, information requests or complaints, please contact New Era Logistics management.
